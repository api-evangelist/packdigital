# Pack Digital Documentation

> Pack Digital is the digital experience platform purpose-built for Shopify Hydrogen. Build, manage, and optimize headless storefronts with visual content management, A/B testing, and developer-friendly APIs.

For the complete documentation in markdown format, see: https://docs.packdigital.com/llms-full.txt
